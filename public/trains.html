Train Editor

GTA Traffic Train Editor

Tune train speeds and braking distances per route. Swap carriage compositions, toggle station stops and ping-pong routing. Export clean traintracks.xml and trains.xml files ready for your mod.

Each route (trains1–12.dat) runs a train group at a fixed speed. Adjust speed, braking distance, station stops, and train type per route. trains1 and trains12 are the main open freight lines (faster by default). trains4 is the metro line.
