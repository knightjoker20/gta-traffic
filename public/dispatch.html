How witnessing works: When a crime with MustBeWitnessed occurs, the game scans nearby NPCs. If any have CanWitnessCrimes and WillCallLawEnforcement, the crime is reported and the wanted level triggers dispatch.meta. Crimes with a Hear Distance can be witnessed by sound alone within that radius — no line-of-sight required.
