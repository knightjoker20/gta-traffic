Vehicle Flags Reference

Complete guide to GTA V model, handling, damage, advanced, and meta flags — how they work, how to decode them, and a searchable database of all known flags.

What Vehicle Flags Are

Vehicle flags, or just flags, are essentially attributes that can be toggled on or off on a per-vehicle basis. In GTA 5 they come in four hex-encoded varieties — Model, Handling, Damage, and Advanced — plus a set of string-based Meta flags in vehicles.meta. Each hex variety has 32 flags associated with it. An example is tyres_can_clip, a handling flag which, when on, allows cars to have a smoother ride over bumps when tyres with larger sidewalls are applied. It's either on or off — there's no partial effect.

How Flags Are Applied (Hex Varieties)

Within the handling data for every vehicle there are four lines with an associated hex string. These four data points (strModelFlags, strHandlingFlags, strDamageFlags, strAdvancedFlags) correspond to the four hex flag varieties. The numbers tell the game which individual flags are active for any given vehicle.

Each hex string is 8 characters representing a 32-bit number. Each hex digit (position) controls 4 flags. The digit value determines which of those 4 flags are active:

  • 1 = 1st flag   2 = 2nd flag   3 = 1st & 2nd flags
  • 4 = 3rd flag   5 = 1st & 3rd   6 = 2nd & 3rd   7 = all three
  • 8 = 4th flag   9–F = combinations of all four
  • F = all 4 flags in that position active

Positions are counted right to left, and leading zeros are omitted in the file (e.g. 00020006 is written as 20006).

Worked Example

To activate reduced_mod_mass: 2nd flag in position 1 → 00000002

To activate has_kers: 3rd flag in position 1 → 00000004

To activate tyres_can_clip: 2nd flag in position 5 → 00020000

All three together → 00020006 (written in file as 20006)

000 200 06
← positions 8 through 1, right to left

This would appear in handling.meta as: <strHandlingFlags>20006</strHandlingFlags>

Advanced Flags

Advanced flags appear to be a way for Rockstar to experiment with different car behaviours outside the usual three varieties. New advanced flags crop up from time to time when vehicles are added to the game with them activated. Their inclusion on cars has often led to undesirable behaviour — especially in early days — making cars worse to drive compared to having the flags deactivated. Their overall effects have sometimes changed across game updates to make them less drastic.

Meta Flags

Meta flags are different from the four hex flag types. They appear as text strings in vehicles.meta and control aspects like whether the vehicle is a bus, whether it has liveries, what type of driver spawns in it, and many other AI and world behaviour settings. They are not encoded in a hex value — instead each is listed as a named flag individually. Use the database below to search the full list.
