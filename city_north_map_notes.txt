GTA-Traffic.com V1.3 City / North Popcycle Maps

This update changes only the Popcycle visual-map assets,
coordinate calibration, and map layer configuration.

Primary map layers:
- City / South Los Santos: 1681 x 1243
- North Country: 1752 x 1215

Automatic behavior:
- City schedules open the City / South map.
- County schedules open the North Country map.
- The right focused panel crops the same regional image.

Coordinate calibration:
- City feature matches: 314
- City inliers: 225
- County feature matches: 253
- County inliers: 156

The schedule pins, focus crops, and selection boxes were
transformed from the previous calibrated maps. They remain
approximate visual references, not official Rockstar polygons.
